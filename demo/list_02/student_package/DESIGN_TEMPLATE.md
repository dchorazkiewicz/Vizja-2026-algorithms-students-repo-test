# Algorithm design template — List 02

Complete the following for each task.

## Contract

What are the input assumptions and required postconditions?

## State and invariant

What does the important state mean during execution? What remains true after every iteration or recursive step?

## Termination

Why does the loop or recursion terminate?

## Data access

Which elements are read and in what pattern?

## Data movement

How many writes, shifts, or swaps should the algorithm perform?

## Stability

Is stability required? If so, why does equal-key ordering remain unchanged?

## Complexity

State the relevant best, average and worst cases. Identify the dominant operation.

## Auxiliary space

How much additional storage grows with the input?

## Pseudocode

Give a language-independent description of the algorithm.
